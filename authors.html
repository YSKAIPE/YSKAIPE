Nick Conenna

Founding author

Writes across literary fiction, speculative fiction, and nonfiction — the primary voice behind the YSKAIPE catalog to date. His work returns often to the same questions from different angles: what a standard is actually for, what a boundary crossing costs, and how far a species can climb before it has to answer for what it's built.

Publishes under Peaking Waters LLC, with a reserved imprint — Spaceship Earth — for space-related titles.

Dee Conenna

Author

Author of The Tides of Battery Island, a dual-timeline historical novel set on the North Carolina coast, and Don't Make It Weird, a psycho-comedy about the moment self-awareness stops helping and starts getting in the way.

A collaborator across the house's day-to-day work, and one of YSKAIPE's founding contributors.
