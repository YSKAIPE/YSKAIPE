Authors

YSKAIPE is a small, founder-led house. Every book has a real name on the cover, and every one goes through the same editing standard before it reaches you.

Still a small house

We haven't become a large traditional publisher, and we're not trying to. Staying small means we can keep every series coherent from book to book and actually read what readers say about what we put out. New voices join carefully, and only when the fit with readers is real.
