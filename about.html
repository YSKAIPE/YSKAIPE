What YSKAIPE Means

An escape, every time you open one.

Say the name out loud: y'scape. That's the whole idea. Every series we publish is a ticket somewhere else — a different coastline, a different country, a different life for a few hundred pages — built for readers who want out for a while, not a lecture on why they should.

The category romance imprints of the last century figured this out first: readers don't buy an author, they buy a promise — a setting, a mood, a feeling they can find again next month. BookTok and Kindle Unlimited didn't invent that model, they just proved how fast it can move. We're building the same way: one clear promise per series, delivered on every single book. If it sells and readers keep coming back for more, that's the whole scoreboard. We don't need the credit — we need the loop to close.

Eight escapes, eight destinations

Every series is built around a real place, or a real kind of place, because armchair travel is one of the oldest reasons people read. Here's where each one takes you, and who it's for.

Historical Fiction · Dual Timeline

The Tidewater Novels — Coastal Carolina

You land on a barrier island off the Cape Fear, half in 1954 and half in now. For readers who travel to find their own history, not just someone else's.

Family Saga · Multigenerational Estate

The Cortlandt Inheritance — New York, Geneva, Lake Como

Private jets, family lawyers, and estates on three continents. For readers who want the international-money fantasy with real consequences attached.

Coastal Magical Realism

The Evertide — a new coastline every book

Outer Banks. West of Ireland. A Norwegian fjord town. Each book moves to a new real-feeling coast where something isn't fully explainable. For readers who want wonder without leaving Earth.

Cozy Mystery · Coastal Town

The Lowcountry Mysteries — Savannah & Charleston harbor country

A harbor town where the accents change with the cruise ship schedule. For readers who want a puzzle, a porch, and a guarantee that nothing here will actually hurt them.

Speculative Fiction · Found Family

Second Save — remote outposts, worldwide

An Arctic research station. A jungle relay post. A crew getting one more shot at each other, somewhere the map runs thin. For readers who want hope with their stakes.

Contemporary Romance

The Ferry Crossing — Mediterranean, Scandinavian, and Asian waterways

A Greek island hop. A Kowloon crossing at dusk. A fjord ferry in the rain. Each standalone rides a different real ferry route to its own second-chance love story. For readers who want to feel like they left the country for an afternoon.

Romantic Suspense · Family Secrets

Still Water — quiet European capitals

Vienna. Lisbon. Copenhagen. Cities that look calm on the surface with a current running underneath. For readers who want tension that resolves into trust, not dread.

Why it works this way

Every series answers to its readers, not to us. We watch what Kindle Unlimited readers actually finish, what they leave five-star reviews for, and what they come back to buy again next month — then we build more of that, in a place worth visiting. No series survives on our say-so. It survives because readers keep opening the door.
