Before & After

From one novel to a house of series

In 2025–2026 YSKAIPE began as a small independent press with a literary sensibility and a single featured historical novel. Two years later it is a multi-series commercial house built deliberately around the categories that sustain long-term reader loyalty and real financial independence for the people who write them.

2025 – EARLY 2026
The original face
A quiet, elegant site. One featured novel — The Tides of Battery Island — and a small catalog of literary, speculative, and nonfiction works. Beautiful. Intentional. And financially precarious for a two-person operation that wanted to write full-time without constant money fear.
MID 2026
The decision
We looked at the data without romance. Romance, romantasy, cozy mystery, and related commercial lanes were where series loyalty, KU page reads, and sustainable income actually lived. We decided to build there — deliberately, at high volume, with franchise architecture from the first outline — while keeping the original literary titles available and respected.
2026 – 2027
The build
Dual writing systems. Shared story bibles. AI-assisted drafting with rigorous human editing and voice control. Aggressive but sustainable release cadence. First Blackthorn book. First romantasy court. First Harbor & Hearth mystery. Email list from day one. Covers and packaging treated as seriously as the prose.
2027 – 2028
The compounding
Backlist began to work. Series read-through proved out. International readers found the books (we had written for them from the start). New titles in existing worlds outperformed standalones. The original literary catalog remained, but the financial engine was now the commercial series. Money anxiety receded. Writing time expanded. The house had a real face.
NOW — 2028
The current house
Seven active series. Forty-plus titles. A clear publishing identity: independent, series-first, internationally considerate, built for franchise longevity. We still write from the soul — but the soul is no longer required to also carry the entire financial weight of two lives. That was the point.

What we optimized for

  • Series architecture over one-off brilliance
  • Reader retention and binge behavior over pure prestige
  • International emotional accessibility
  • Sustainable production systems (two people + smart tools)
  • Worlds with enough depth to support spin-offs, adaptations, and long-term fandom
  • Financial independence so the writing could eventually be freer, not more constrained
