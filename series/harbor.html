Harbor & Hearth Mysteries

A working harbor town that draws tourists, sailors, and secrets from around the world. Low violence, high heart, and an amateur sleuth who keeps finding the quiet crimes between the tourist seasons. Six books and ongoing.

Designed for both series binge readers and those who prefer mostly-standalone entries. International visitors and residents are woven into the fabric of the town from the beginning.
