The Tides of Battery Island

Forthcoming — final research trip to Southport underway

Hurricane Hazel put a cargo schooner on the oyster shoals behind Battery Island in October of 1954 — and something went down with her that never made it into the harbor log. Seventy years later, Charlotte Lynch comes home to Southport to bury her grandfather and finds a codicil naming her family heir to the island itself, and a neighbor who makes it clear the marsh has been waiting for someone to come asking.
