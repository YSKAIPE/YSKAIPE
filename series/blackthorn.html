The Blackthorn Syndicate

Global crime families. Morally gray antiheroes. The kind of loyalty and love that leaves permanent marks. Five books available. Two more announced. A universe built to expand.

The World

The Blackthorn name carries weight on three continents. What began as one family’s empire has become a network of alliances, rivalries, and blood debts that stretch from the docks of Naples to the high-rises of Mexico City and the quiet power centers of New York.

Each book focuses on a different power couple inside the larger web — men and women who would burn cities for each other, and sometimes do. The series is designed so new readers can enter at multiple points while long-time readers get the full tapestry.

Why it works internationally

  • Multiple cultural and geographic centers of power
  • Characters and conflicts that travel across borders
  • Emotional language that does not rely on one country’s tropes alone
  • Clear series architecture for translation and adaptation
