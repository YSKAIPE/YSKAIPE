Eight escapes. Pick your destination.

Every series is built around a real place, or a real kind of place — because that's half of what a good escape is. Find the destination that matches the mood you're in, and start there.

THE TIDEWATER
NOVELS
Historical Fiction · Coastal Carolina, Dual Timeline

The Tidewater Novels

You land on a barrier island off the Cape Fear, half in 1954 and half in now. Each book opens on a wreck the record never accounted for — storms, salvage, and inheritance — and a present-day descendant who has to decide what to do about what the past left behind. For readers who travel to find their own history, not just someone else's.

Start with: The Tides of Battery Island — Book One, forthcoming

THE CORTLANDT
INHERITANCE
Family Saga · New York, Geneva, Lake Como

The Cortlandt Inheritance

Private jets, family lawyers, and an estate on three continents. Five core novels, two announced sequels, following one family's fortune as it passes — and is contested — across generations, from a Manhattan boardroom to a villa on Lake Como. For readers who want the international-money fantasy with real consequences attached.

Reading order: The First Deed → The Codicil → House of Cortlandt → The Long Ledger → Inheritance Tax

Coastal Magical Realism · A New Coastline Every Book

The Evertide

The Outer Banks. The west of Ireland. A fjord town in Norway. Four books deep, each one moving to a new real-feeling coastline where the uncanny sits just under the surface of ordinary life. For readers who want wonder without leaving Earth.

Reading order: The Tide Gate → Salt and Starlight → The Drowned Orchard → What the Marsh Keeps

THE LOWCOUNTRY
MYSTERIES
Cozy Mystery · Savannah & Charleston Harbor Country

The Lowcountry Mysteries

A working harbor town where the accents change with the cruise ship schedule. Six books and counting, low violence, high heart, and an amateur sleuth who keeps stumbling into the town's quieter crimes. For readers who want a puzzle, a porch, and a guarantee that nothing here will actually hurt them.

Start with: The Salt Marker — then read in order or as mostly standalones
